Redline

Hold the redline.

The road winds through the trees. Steer with ← → or A / D — on touch, hold either side of the screen. Brake with ↓ or S — on touch, hold both sides at once. Weave through traffic; the longer you last, the faster you go, and the harder the bends shove you outward. The day moves too: dusk falls as you drive, and at night the road runs on headlights and tail-lights until dawn.

Metres pay one point each — but shave past a car with less than half a width to spare and it pays up to 100. Chain close passes within three seconds and they multiply, up to x5. The brave line outscores the safe one — and braking buys room in a pinch, but every second spent slow is metres and chain you are not banking. Pace is yours to spend.
